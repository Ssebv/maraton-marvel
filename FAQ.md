## The basics

### How much does it cost?
Nothing. No ads, no purchases, no account needed.

### Do I need to download anything?
No: it opens in your browser at [ssebv.github.io/maraton-marvel](https://ssebv.github.io/maraton-marvel/?lang=en). If you install it, it gets its own icon on your home screen and works like any other app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → *Install app*.

### Does it work offline?
Yes, once you've opened it with a connection. You can check off what you watch and browse your marathon with no signal. Trailers and live lookups (such as which service has a title today) need a connection.

### Is it in English?
Yes. It follows your phone's language, and you can switch between English and Spanish in Settings. Posters are still the Spanish-language editions for now.

## Your progress

### Where is what I check off saved?
On your phone or computer, inside the browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server and you don't need an account.

### Can I lose it?
Yes, in two cases: if you clear your browser data, or if you use **Safari without installing the app**, because Safari deletes what a website stores if you don't open it for 7 days. To avoid that:

1. **Install it** (see above). Once installed, Safari won't delete it.
2. **Back it up now and then:** Settings › Your progress › *Download backup*. It saves everything: what you've watched, episodes, notes, lists and reading.

### How do I move it to another phone or my computer?
In Settings › Your progress:

- **Quick:** *Copy code* on your usual phone, then *Load code* on the other one and paste it. It carries what you've watched.
- **Full:** *Download backup* on one and *Restore backup* on the other. It carries everything.

Heads up: loading a code or a backup **replaces** whatever is on that device.

### I want to start over
Settings › *Start over*. If you think you might regret it, download a backup first.

## The order

### What order is it in?
The order in which **the story happens**, not the release order. That's why *Captain America: The First Avenger* (World War II) comes before *Iron Man*. The order was reviewed with the community and keeps improving with your reports.

### Why is the X-Men saga split in two?
Because it has two timelines. In *X-Men: Days of Future Past*, Wolverine travels back to 1973 and changes history: everything after that happens on a new timeline. If everything were ordered together, you'd see characters die before you meet them.

### I don't have time for everything. What's the bare minimum?
In **Filters**, turn on the **Express route**: it keeps only what's essential before *Avengers: Doomsday*. You can also turn on *No optionals* (drops the skippable stuff) or *No series* (movies and specials only).

### Will I make it to Doomsday?
The app looks at your real pace (how much you've watched in recent weeks) and tells you whether you'll make it. For a plan, go to **More › Schedule**, pick which days and how long you watch, and it tells you what's up each day and when you'll finish. You can add it to your calendar.

### I think a title is in the wrong place
Tell us! [Open an order issue](https://github.com/Ssebv/maraton-marvel/issues/new?template=1-orden.yml) and say what you'd move and why.

## Where to watch and read

### How does it know which service has each title?
From [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/) data, which changes often. In Settings you pick your country (19 of them, including the United States, Mexico and Spain) and each title page tells you whether it's included with a subscription, free with ads, or rent/buy only.

Wrong in your country? [Let us know](https://github.com/Ssebv/maraton-marvel/issues/new?template=4-plataforma.yml).

### Why aren't there links to watch for free?
Because they'd be piracy, and this app only points to legal sources. Those links aren't allowed in the community either.

### Can I read the comics in the app?
Yes, if you have the file (CBZ, ZIP, PDF or the pages as images): on the comic's page, *Read here*. The file stays on your device and isn't uploaded anywhere. If you don't have it, the page tells you where to read it legally.

## Spoilers

### Can the app spoil things for me?
We try hard not to. Episode synopses are blurred until you tap them. And with **Settings › Spoilers › No spoilers** on, synopses, post-credit scenes and episode titles are hidden for everything you haven't watched yet.

## Friends and community

### Can I do the marathon with friends?
Yes:

- **Share your profile** (Profile › *Share profile*) and anyone can see how far you are, without being able to change anything.
- **Duel mode:** paste a friend's profile link and see who's ahead.
- **Marathon club:** join with a code from a friend and compete on a leaderboard, with medals and comments on every title.

### Will there be communities inside the app?
Yes, they're on the way: your own groups, forums for every movie and episode that hide spoilers from anyone who hasn't watched it, challenges and following people. Meanwhile, the conversation is in [Discussions](https://github.com/Ssebv/maraton-marvel/discussions).

### How can I help?
- [Open an issue](https://github.com/Ssebv/maraton-marvel/issues/new/choose) if something breaks, a title is out of order, a translation is off, or you have an idea.
- Share the app with anyone on the same marathon.
- Read the [community guidelines](comunidad/normas.md) before joining in.
