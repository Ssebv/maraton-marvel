## September 2026

### Week of the 22nd
- **A new Home, streaming-app style.** When you open the app you see what's next in your marathon, big, with its trailer and a button to mark it watched. Below are rows for "Continue watching", "Up next", "Toward Doomsday", the top 10 and your favorites.
- **Home works offline:** images are saved, and the next title is already loaded when you mark one watched.
- **A tidier toolbar:** now it's just three buttons (Search, Filters and More), and each option explains what it does.
- **Multiverse revamp:** better-looking planets, each with its own features, and the links between Earths explained.
- **Better English:** posters are now the English-language ones, and Earth names and when each title takes place show in English too.

### Week of the 15th
- **Your month:** a monthly recap in Profile with hours, titles and active days.
- **29 achievements** (up from 10), including X-Men ones, showing what's left for each.
- **"Where am I":** in the list, a pill shows which saga and era you're in, and an index jumps anywhere.
- **Your calendar on Home**, with each day's stars and reviews.
- **Rate right after marking,** and undo if you slipped.
- **Forgiving search:** finds the title even with typos.
- The app starts up faster.

### Week of the 11th
- **Collapsible sagas and eras:** anything you've fully watched folds away into a poster carousel.
- **Episodes grouped by season** on every series page.
- The poster "flies" into the title page when you open it, and animations feel more natural.
- On phones, the bars hide when you scroll down and come back when you scroll up.

### Week of the 1st
- **Spoiler-free mode:** hides synopses, post-credit scenes and episode titles for everything you haven't watched yet.
- **Light or dark theme**, or follow your phone.
- **Gestures in the installed app:** swipe from the edge to go back, pull a title page down to close it.
- The app tells you when a new version is out.
- If you have a marathon session today, the app icon shows it.

## August 2026

### Week of the 25th
- **The app speaks English.** Plus **19 countries**: each title's streaming service follows yours.
- **Marathon schedule:** pick days, length and time, and the app tells you what's up each day, when you'll finish and whether you'll make Doomsday. You can add it to your calendar.
- **Marathon calendar:** a month where each day lights up with what you watched.
- **Comic reader:** open your own files (CBZ, CBR, PDF or images) inside the app, and every comic shows where to read it legally.
- **Mark a whole season** in one tap.
- **The X-Men saga is ordered by its two timelines,** so nobody dies before they show up.
- A filter for what you can watch tonight without renting anything.

### Week of the 18th
- **The animation vault:** Marvel's animated series on Disney+, trackable episode by episode.
- **Backup to a file,** so you don't lose your progress.
- **Marathon diary** and when you'll finish at your pace.
- Search by actor, director or year.
- Colors for each universe, like the X-Men, the TVA or the Zombies, and an optional sound when you check something off.
- Easier-to-read text and easier-to-tap buttons.

### Week of the 16th: the beginning
- **Marvel & X-Men Marathon is born:** all of Marvel and X-Men in chronological order, with trackable episodes.
- Countdown to *Avengers: Doomsday*, stats, achievements and streaks.
- **Multiverse:** each Earth as a planet, with its titles.
- Trailers, spoiler-free synopses, where to watch and post-credit scenes on every title page.
- **With friends:** shareable profile, duel mode and a marathon club with a leaderboard.
- Your own lists, a timeline, a gallery and a cinema mode for the TV.
- Installable on your phone and works offline.
